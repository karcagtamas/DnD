\section{Appearance}

Kalea Corven is a twenty-one-year-old woman of slim build, with striking blue eyes and unusual grey hair. Despite her relatively young age, there is something guarded about her appearance, as though she has learned to be constantly aware of her surroundings.

She wears dark travelling clothes reinforced with practical leather armour, accompanied by sturdy leather boots. A long, worn black cloak with a damaged hood covers most of her clothing and allows her to conceal her appearance when necessary. Its condition reflects the hardships of life on the road rather than the comfortable existence she once knew.

Her clothes are chosen for practicality, protection, and anonymity. She avoids luxurious accessories and anything that might reveal her noble origins. Nevertheless, certain gestures, habits, and mannerisms occasionally betray the education and upbringing she received.

\section{Behaviour}

Kalea is intelligent, curious, and analytical. She has a strong desire to learn and continuously seeks opportunities to expand her knowledge and improve her abilities. She prefers to observe and understand a situation before taking action, often considering several possible outcomes before committing to a decision.

Her noble upbringing has made her familiar with the customs and behaviour of the upper classes. However, she no longer identifies with the values associated with that world. She strongly dislikes arrogance, exploitation, and the abuse of authority, particularly when those in positions of power take advantage of people who cannot defend themselves.

Although she has ambitious ideals, Kalea understands that changing society cannot happen overnight. She is willing to pursue smaller, achievable goals if they bring tangible improvements to people's lives.

She is cautious about revealing personal information and does not easily trust strangers. Her current circumstances require discretion, and she knows that a careless word or an unexpected encounter could expose her identity.

Despite her careful nature, she can sometimes be stubborn when confronted with injustice. Her convictions may lead her to take risks even when remaining uninvolved would be the safer choice.

\section{Background}

\subsection{Noble Origins}

Kalea was born into a noble family and grew up surrounded by the privileges and expectations that came with her position. Her upbringing provided her with an extensive education and access to subjects that were largely unavailable to people of lower social standing.

She studied history, politics, and the structure of society, developing an understanding of how power is maintained and how decisions made by the upper classes affect everyone beneath them.

Her education also introduced her to strategic thinking and the importance of understanding people's motivations. These lessons would eventually become some of her most valuable skills.

However, the more she learned about the world beyond her family's comfortable existence, the more difficult it became for her to accept the inequalities upon which their position depended.

\subsection{The Decision to Leave}

Kalea eventually chose to abandon her former life and escape from her family. The details and circumstances surrounding her departure remain closely guarded secrets.

Leaving meant giving up the security, resources, and social position she had been born into. It also meant becoming someone who had to rely on her own abilities rather than her family name.

She now lives in hiding, constantly aware that her family might discover her whereabouts. She avoids drawing unnecessary attention to herself and tries not to leave any obvious traces of her former identity.

Although she has left her old life behind, she has not abandoned the desire to learn. On the contrary, she continues to study and train whenever circumstances allow, believing that knowledge and preparation will be essential to whatever she intends to accomplish.

\subsection{A Different Purpose}

Kalea's experiences and education have shaped a clear long-term ambition: she wants to change the rigid social structure that divides people according to birth, wealth, and status.

She believes that a person's worth should not be determined by the family they were born into or the position they occupy in society. Privilege should not grant someone the right to exploit others, and those who suffer under an unjust system should not be denied the opportunity to improve their lives.

Her ultimate goal is to help reshape society into one where people are treated as equals.

For now, however, such an ambition is far beyond her immediate reach. She therefore focuses on smaller objectives: helping oppressed people, supporting those without power or influence, and holding members of the privileged classes accountable when they abuse their position.

Kalea knows that she cannot change the world alone. She must continue learning, become stronger, and find people willing to stand alongside her.

Her journey is still at its beginning. She has escaped the life that was chosen for her, but she has yet to discover how much she will have to sacrifice to build the world she believes in.
