# Temple of Eternal Flame

- Northwest quarter of the ancient dwarven fortress
  - Served as the manufacturing center
  - Beautful and deadly armors and weapons
  - Lava from the volcanic rock below provided heat and material
- **Vanifer** saw potential in the complex
  - He wanted to use the forge to rule over the others

## Lava Tubes

- `E1` connects the surface through a series of twisting, turning lava tubes
- The tubes join the underground temple to **Scarlet Moon Hall**
- Numerous side tubes and old mining tunnels branch off into darkness from the main route, which is well lit by torches at periodic intervals

## E1 - Entrance

- An arrow slit is carvbed in the south wall of the lave tube leading to this area
  - 3ft tall, 6 inches wide
  - Behind there is a tunnel leading to `E6`

> Az átjáró ezen a ponton kiszélesedik; a folyosó két oldalán egy-egy tágas fülke nyílik. A fülkék bejáratánál laza kőhalmokból emelt barikádok állnak, amelyeket úgy tájoltak, hogy védelmet nyújtsanak a nyugat felől érkező behatolókkal szemben. A barikádok között húzódó út egy széles, kelet felé leereszkedő lépcsősorhoz vezet.

- 4 *hobgoblins* and 1 *hobgoblin captain* guard here, dressed in flame-red tabards with the symbol of elemental fire on the chest
  - They challange anyone approaching from the west, but the pay little attention to anyone heading to west
- Visitors to east must showe handsign of the fire cult (hands cupped tohether upright, with on pinky finger upward in the middle)
  - Or state: `Fire is eternal!`
  - Otherwise the visitors must convince the *hobgoblins* that they must talk to the temple leaders
    - 2 *hobgoblins* escort the party to `E2`
- Reinforcements:
  - Combat sounds alert guards in `E2`

### E1 - Treasure

- The captain has a belt pouch with 20 gp and a pair of polished bloodstones worth 50 gp each

### E1 - Development

- If the characters defeat these *hobgoblins* and the leave the dungons the area filled with guards from `E10` in a or two hours

## E2 - Columned Chamber

- If the characters did not raise the alarm:

> A folyosó kiszélesedik, és egy kupolás csarnokká nyílik, amelyet négy, tüzes repedésekkel átszőtt obszidiánoszlop tart. A levegő fojtogatóan meleg.

- 2 *razerblasts* keep to the sides of the chamber
  - If the characters come from the fire temple and act like they belong there, they pay little mind
  - If the characters arrive escorted by *hobgoblins* must convince the guards to let them pass
    - If they do, the *razerblasts* escort the party
  - If fight breaks out the *razerblasts* shouts: `Imix defend us`
- If fight happens:

> Az obszidiánoszlopok olyan magmaoszlopokká alakulnak át, amelyek intenzív hőt és fényt sugároznak, amitől vibrál a levegő.

- If a creature moves withing 10 ft of on or more magma pillars for the first time on a turn end its turn there takes 2d10 fire damage
- *razerblasts* have immunity to fire damage

### E2 - Development

- If the guards and leave the area, a replacement comes from `E7` in 2 hours
  - These cultists know the command phrase to activate the columns

## E3 - Niche Hall

> Ennek a sötét folyosónak mindkét oldalán fülkék sorakoznak. Mindegyik fülkében egy árnyalak áll.

- Teh shadowy forms are statues of dwarven warriors, carved long ango as an honor guard for those passing through this area
- Tripwire Trap
  - Across the floor between the westernmost pair of niches
  - Characters with passive Perception 15 or higher notice it automatically
    - Otherwise DC 10 Perception check to search for traps
    - If the wire is tripped, a spring-loaded greatace disguised to look like part of the southhern status snaps across the hallway and makes an attack to the triggerer creature
      - +10 to hit, 1d12+5 slashing damage
      - Alerts teh guards in `E2`
- Secret Door
  - Behind the southeasternmost statue (DC 15 Investigation)
